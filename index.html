8 Landmarks of Shenyang

Shenyang Imperial Palace

One of China's only two surviving imperial palace complexes, built in 1625, blending Manchu and Han architectural styles.

Shenyang Imperial Palace

Also known as the Mukden Palace, it was the palace built and used by Nurhaci and Huang Taiji of the Qing Dynasty. Covering an area of 60,000 square meters, it has 114 ancient buildings with over 500 rooms, and was listed on the World Heritage List in 2004.

Cultural Relics World Heritage

Qipanshan Mountain

Shenyang's iconic natural landscape, famous nationwide for its Ice and Snow World in winter, with beautiful mountains and waters in summer.

Qipanshan Mountain

Qipanshan Scenic Area covers 148 square kilometers, including four core areas: Qipanshan Mountain, Huishan Mountain, Dayangshan Mountain and Xiu Lake. The Ice and Snow World in winter is one of the core landmarks of ice and snow tourism in Northeast China.

Natural Scenery Ice and Snow Tourism

Tiexi Industrial Corridor

Witness to Shenyang's industrial glory as the "eldest son of the republic", now transformed into a cultural and creative technology zone.

Tiexi Industrial Corridor

Tiexi District was once China's largest industrial zone, home to well-known enterprises such as Shenyang Machine Tool and Shenyang Blower Works. Now it preserves industrial relics and has built cultural and tourism landmarks such as the Industrial Museum and Cultural and Creative Park.

Industrial Heritage Urban Renewal

Middle Street

One of the longest commercial pedestrian streets in China, with a 400-year history, blending modern commerce and historical culture.

Middle Street

Middle Street is 1500 meters long, built in 1625, and is Shenyang's earliest commercial center. Now it gathers modern commercial complexes such as Joy City and Hang Lung Plaza, with an annual passenger flow of over 100 million.

Modern City Commercial Landmark

Zhang's Shuai Mansion

The official residence and private mansion of Zhang Zuolin and Zhang Xueliang, featuring a distinctive blend of Chinese and Western architectural styles.

Zhang's Shuai Mansion

Also known as the "Grand Marshal's Mansion", it was built in 1914, covering a total area of 36,000 square meters. It includes Chinese-style quadrangles, Western-style buildings, and Xiaoqing Building, and is an important witness to modern and contemporary history of Northeast China.

Cultural Relics Historical Building

Beiling Park

The location of Zhaoling Mausoleum of the Qing Dynasty, with a 300-year-old ancient pine forest, is Shenyang's largest urban park.

Beiling Park

Beiling Park covers an area of 3.3 million square meters, with the core being Zhaoling Mausoleum, the mausoleum of Huang Taiji of the Qing Dynasty. There are more than 2,000 ancient pine trees in the park, which is an important place for Shenyang citizens to relax and exercise.

Natural Scenery Royal Mausoleum

1905 Cultural and Creative Park

A cultural and creative park transformed from an old factory building, a popular check-in spot combining industrial style and artistic vibe.

1905 Cultural and Creative Park

The predecessor of 1905 Cultural and Creative Park was an old factory building of Shenyang Heavy Machinery Plant, built in 1905. Now it has become a cultural landmark integrating art exhibitions, cultural and creative markets, and livehouses.

Industrial Heritage Cultural and Creative Landmark

K11 Art Mall

A high-end shopping mall integrating art, humanity and nature, a new business card of modern Shenyang.

K11 Art Mall

Shenyang K11 has a total construction area of 260,000 square meters, being the first "art shopping mall" in Northeast China. It has an art exhibition hall, sky garden, etc., deeply integrating shopping and art experience.

Modern City Art and Commerce
